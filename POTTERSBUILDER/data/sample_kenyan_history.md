# Sample: Early 20th-century Kenya (short excerpt)

Kenya's modern political history began taking shape during the early 20th century under the British colonial administration. Land alienation, the construction of the Uganda Railway, and settler agriculture changed traditional economies and social structures. Movements of protest and organisation evolved through the 1920s–1950s, culminating in the Mau Mau uprising (1952–1960), which played a significant role in the push towards independence.

Jomo Kenyatta emerged as a central figure in Kenya's struggle for independence, later becoming the country's first prime minister and president. His political career reflected the complexity of negotiating traditional authority, colonial power, and nationalist politics.
