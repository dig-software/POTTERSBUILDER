# Colonialism in Kenya — Overview (short)

From the late 19th century, what is now Kenya was incorporated into the British Empire. The imposition of colonial administration, the creation of settler enclaves, and the construction of major infrastructure such as the Uganda Railway transformed political and economic life. Colonial rule introduced new land laws and taxation systems, restricted political rights, and prioritized settler agriculture.

Key themes during the colonial period include land alienation, labour migration, the growth of a cash-crop economy, the emergence of new urban centres, and political movements that eventually led to organized nationalist demands for independence.
