# Mau Mau Uprising (1952–1960) — Summary

The Mau Mau uprising was a complex anti-colonial movement, principally in the central highlands, which opposed land dispossession and colonial rule. It involved Kikuyu veterans and other communities who used both political and armed resistance. The British colonial state responded with emergency regulations, mass detentions, and military operations.

Historians debate Mau Mau's nature — whether primarily a nationalist movement, a social revolution, or both — and its legacy remains central to modern Kenyan politics and memory.
